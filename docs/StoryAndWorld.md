# Story and World Overview

## Premise
The archipelago of **Aetheris** was once united by the **Skyspindle**, a floating crystal that balanced the seasons and ocean tides. The crystal shattered, scattering **Shardstones** across the islands and corrupting the weather. You are **The Wayfarer**, a shipwrecked explorer who learns they can attune to the Shardstones and restore the balance.

## Key Themes
- Restoration vs. exploitation of power
- Unity across rival island cultures
- Exploration of ancient ruins and skybound tech

## Factions
- **Harbor League**: Merchant guilds controlling trade routes and docks.
- **Verdant Wardens**: Druidic caretakers of the wild island groves.
- **Emberwrights**: Engineers living in volcanic forges and sky-machine ruins.
- **Mistcallers**: Scholars who preserve history and map sea currents.

## World Structure
- **Starter Island – Driftwake**: Safe tutorial cove, starter quests, tiny lighthouse.
- **City Island – Port Lumen**: Main hub city, markets, guild halls, central quest board.
- **Wild Island – Greenfall**: Dense forest, ruins, Wardens HQ.
- **Forge Island – Cinderreach**: Volcanic biome, lava canals, Emberwrights workshops.
- **Sky Isle – Zephyr Crest**: Floating remnants, skybridge puzzles, final shard.

## Main Story Beats (Act Structure)
1. **Awakening**: Survive the wreck, learn the Skyspindle myth, recover first Shardstone.
2. **Gathering**: Earn faction trust, collect two Shardstones.
3. **Reckoning**: Uncover who sabotaged the Skyspindle and their motives.
4. **Restoration**: Rebuild the core, stabilize weather, and earn peace between factions.

## Tone and Mood
- High-fantasy, light steampunk elements.
- Warm lighting in cities, cool mist in ruins, saturated flora in forests.

## Player Progression
- **Rank 1 – Wayfarer**: Basic gear, beginner quests, accessible zones.
- **Rank 2 – Pathfinder**: Access to vaults, advanced travel routes.
- **Rank 3 – Skybinder**: Final island access, upgraded resource pack items.
