# Islands and Cities

## Driftwake (Starter Island)
- **Biome**: Beach, shallow reef, sparse jungle.
- **Landmarks**: Shipwreck cove, lighthouse, fisher hut.
- **Build Notes**: Compact routes, clear signposting, safe mobs.

## Port Lumen (City Island)
- **Biome**: Coastal city with canals.
- **Districts**:
  - **Harbor Market**: Quest board, vendors, inn.
  - **Guild Row**: Faction halls, ranking banners.
  - **Cathedral Plaza**: Skyspindle memorial, puzzle vault entrance.
- **Build Notes**: Use lanterns for guidance, provide quick travel paths.

## Greenfall (Wild Island)
- **Biome**: Dense forest, cliffs, vine bridges.
- **Landmarks**: Warden grove, ruins, waterfall cave.
- **Build Notes**: Hidden paths, parkour but safe fallbacks.

## Cinderreach (Forge Island)
- **Biome**: Volcano, basalt shelves, lava rivers.
- **Landmarks**: Emberwright forge, steamworks, scorched rail.
- **Build Notes**: Heat hazard signage, safe paths with iron grates.

## Zephyr Crest (Sky Isle)
- **Biome**: Floating islands, cloud bridges.
- **Landmarks**: Skybridge puzzle, shard altar, wind towers.
- **Build Notes**: Elytra/glide segments with safety nets.

## City Landmark Checklist
- Clearly visible town hall or central landmark in each hub.
- One fast-travel route per island (boat dock or portal).
- Color-coded banners for factions and questlines.
